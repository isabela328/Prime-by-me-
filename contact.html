Contact and support

Have questions? We are here to help.
Check out the FAQs below or open the chat anytime for instant support.

Does it work without internet?

Some features may require an internet connection to synchronize customer information and payment records. Connect to the internet to access the latest information.

How to start using the app?

Start by registering your business information, adding your customers, and recording their credit purchases and payments. You can then use the customer list to review outstanding balances.

Do payment reminders arrive automatically?

You can use the reminder feature to contact customers about pending payments. Automatic reminders depend on the features and services configured for your account.

Can I export my reports?

The Reports section can be used to review business information. PDF or Excel exports will work when those features are connected to your application.

What if a customer doesn't pay?

Review the customer's outstanding balance, check the payment due date, and contact the customer with a friendly reminder. Keep the payment information updated in your records.
